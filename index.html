Health Tips of the Week

Small habits, real results

Quick, do-today guidance you can pick up between classes, commutes and work shifts.

Two-minute sunscreen rule

Apply sunscreen 15 minutes before heading out so it sets. Rushing out the door is the #1 reason protection fails.

Pat dry, don't rub

After ligo, pat skin dry — especially folds and between toes. Friction plus moisture is how fungal infections start.

Water before coffee

Start the day with a glass of water. Heat and sweating dehydrate you faster than you feel — thirst is a late signal.

Check the UV index

In the Philippines the UV index often hits 9–11 by noon. If it's 3 or higher, exposed skin needs sunscreen.

Never pick a pimple

Squeezing pushes bacteria deeper and leaves marks that last months. Spot-treat, cover, and let it heal.

Wash hands at key moments

Before eating, after the CR, and after handling money or jeepney rails — 20 seconds with soap each time.

Sun Protection Guide

Triple S every morning: Sunscreen, Shade, Surface coverage

Filipino skin tans easily and hyperpigmentation is common — but that doesn't mean you need to fear the sun. You need a system.

  • 1Sunscreen: SPF 30–50 with PA+++, two finger-lengths for the face and neck.
  • 2Shade: Plan walks before 10 AM or after 3 PM; carry an umbrella or a cap.
  • 3Surface: UPF-rated or tightly woven long sleeves for long commutes and field work.
